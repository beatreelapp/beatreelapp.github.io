Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

BeatReel is a music video and video captions maker that runs in your browser and in the BeatReel Android app. This page explains what data is used and why. We collect as little as possible and we do not sell data.

1. Your songs and videos

Videos are created on your device. We do not receive or store the videos you make.

When you use an AI feature (for example Auto captions, Auto lyrics or Auto style), the audio of your song or video, or the recognised words, is sent to our server so the AI can process it. The server sends the result back and does not keep your audio or your words after the request ends. This processing runs on Cloudflare Workers AI. If you do not use these features, nothing from your files is sent.

2. Anonymous usage counts

The app counts simple events such as “app opened” or “video exported”, per day, without any personal data. No names, no email addresses and no IP addresses are stored with these counts.

3. Error reports

When something fails, the app may send a short technical report: where the error happened, the error message, the app version and the browser/phone type. It contains no personal data and no IP address. We keep only the latest reports to fix bugs.

4. Daily limits

To share the free AI credits fairly, the app creates a random device ID that is saved in your browser. To limit abuse, our server also uses a scrambled one-day code made from your network address. The address itself is not stored, and the code changes every day.

5. Payments (BeatReel Pro)

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details and billing information go directly to Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We keep only your Pro code, the Stripe subscription reference and its status, so we can unlock Pro on up to 3 of your devices.

6. Data saved on your device

Your settings, your coins and your Pro code are saved in your browser’s local storage. You can delete them at any time by clearing the site data or uninstalling the app.

7. Other services

  • Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts, which can see that your browser requested them.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The server is run on Cloudflare.

8. Children

BeatReel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Your rights

Because we do not keep personal data linked to you, there is usually nothing to access or delete. If you have a question, or want your Pro code or subscription record removed, write to us.
