How it works

  1. Choose “Video captions”Then pick a video from your phone.
  2. Tap “Auto captions”The AI listens to the speech and writes the words with their timing. It also picks a caption style that fits.
  3. Pick a background and downloadChoose one of 10 calm backgrounds, check the words, then tap Download.

What you get

  • Words timed by AI, in many languages
  • Caption looks: box, pill, glass and word highlight
  • An emoji that matches each line
  • 10 calm backgrounds with your video in a clean frame
  • Optional auto zoom on key words

Questions

Is BeatReel free?

Yes. Free videos have a small BeatReel watermark, last up to 30 seconds and save in 540p or 720p. You also get 3 free AI runs a day (Auto lyrics or Auto captions). Pro costs €4.99 a month: no watermark, Full HD 1080p, longer videos and unlimited AI. You can cancel anytime.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The video is made on your device. For Auto captions, only the sound is sent to our server so the AI can write the words, and the server does not keep it.

How long can my video be?

Auto captions work on up to 10 minutes per run. Free videos are saved up to 30 seconds; Pro saves longer videos.
